## Data and display behavior

The new addresses are independently sourced durable facts in `reviewed-places.json`, with actual review timestamps and stable location IDs. An address can be reviewed before a Google place ID exists. The registry validates a street address, Australian locality/state/country and source, and preserves every existing place/location identity. Source-scoped business provenance confirms only the reviewed fields.

Address-only cafes display locality links, participate in street/postcode search, obey state/cafe filters and keep all branches visible alongside any existing rated branches. Google Maps opens an address search; these records do not imply a confirmed Google place ID, rating or coordinate. No provider discovery or geocoding was performed, and no new provider coordinates, addresses or ratings were stored.
